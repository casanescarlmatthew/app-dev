# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things
## Overview
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction horror series created by the Duffer Brothers. It premiered on *Netflix* in 2016 and has become one of the most popular shows on the platform.
## Favorite Characters
1. **Eleven** - A girl with psychokinetic abilities
2. **Mike Wheeler** - The leader of the group
3. **Dustin Henderson** - The comic relief with a big heart
4. **Jim Hopper** - The tough but caring police chief
